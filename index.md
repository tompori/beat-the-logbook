# Beat the Logbook

A gym logbook and food tracker for iPhone. Log your sets and meals, see your trends, and beat what you did last time.

- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)

## Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tompori/beat-the-logbook/issues). Please don't include personal data you'd rather keep private: issues are public.

If you're testing through TestFlight, the quickest way to report something is to take a screenshot in the app. TestFlight then offers to send it with a note.
