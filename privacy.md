# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

Beat the Logbook keeps your data on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it doesn't track you.

## What the app stores

Your workouts, sets, foods, meals, recipes, targets and body details (such as height, age and weight) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. The developer can't see them, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, the app **reads your body weight** from Apple Health. It uses it to show your weight trend, to calculate calorie targets, and to count your body weight in exercises like dips and pull-ups. It never writes anything to Apple Health, and Health data never leaves your device. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

## Backups you export

Settings → Data → Export Data creates a backup file of your data. It goes only where you choose to save or send it, and is then under your control.

## TestFlight

If you're testing a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports, basic device information, and any screenshots and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's own privacy policy. They're used only to fix problems in the app.

## Changes

If the app ever starts storing or sending data differently, for example syncing between your devices through your own iCloud, this page will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tompori/beat-the-logbook/issues). Please don't include personal data you'd rather keep private: issues are public.
